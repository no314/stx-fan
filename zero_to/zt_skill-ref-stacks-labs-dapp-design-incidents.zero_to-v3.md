# Incidents behind the design rules

Dated stories, each with the rule it supports. Read when a rule looks arbitrary. Facts here are not rules; the rules are in SKILL.md.

- **2026-09-03, Zero to Signing, cycle bar wrong in four ways.** `Math.round` showed "100% done" for roughly the last 10 blocks of a 2100-block cycle; the marker was clamped to 3 to 97 percent so at one block from the end the caret pointed about 60 blocks earlier than its number; the prepare phase was a label suffix with no geometry; the ends row labelled the next cycle's first block as the end. A first fix anchored the prepare label to its percentage and overprinted the end label at 95.2 percent. 116 functional checks passed throughout; every defect was found by a person reading screenshots at specific blocks. Rules: chain-timing widgets; functional assertions do not see design.
- **2026-09-03, Zero to Signing, PRD said 48 cycles maximum.** The chain says 96. The field now validates 1 to 96, prefills 48 and binds the copy to the entered value. Rule: every chain number is unverified until checked against the chain.
- **2026-08-27, sBTC Deposit Recovery, seed detector false positive.** "Any 12 short lowercase words" discarded an ordinary support message; the report came from a real user paste. Replaced by the BIP39 run rule. Rule: inputs that could receive key material.
- **2026-10-09, sBTC Deposit Recovery, outcome first.** The 2026-10-09 build moved the verdict to the front and folded the evidence into a collapsed list, and replaced protocol vocabulary ("signer votes", "HTTP 201") with plain words; the audience had moved from support staff to depositors. Candidate: outcome first; rule: register layering.
- **2026-09-21, sBTC gas swap, ledger defects.** A screenshot caught an uppercase transform on "sBTC" and decimals not aligning on the point; 171 functional checks did not. Rule: visual pass from a committed script.
- **2026-09 to 2026-10, sBTC gas swap, one payment two names.** "Network fee" described both the tier the user repays and what the sponsor pays the chain until "miner fee" was introduced; a harness check now forbids the mix. Rule: glossary, one word per object.
- **2026-10-04 to 2026-10-14, the no-key header statement removed.** BNS Lookup (owner request, 2026-10-04), sBTC gas swap (connects wallets, 2026-09-21) and the tax export ("as on BNS Lookup", 2026-10-14) all dropped the statement; the homepage never had it. Only sBTC Deposit Recovery carries it. Result: the statement is a candidate, not a rule.
- **2026-10-18, Zero To BNS, ligatures.** JetBrains Mono's contextual ligatures merged the two slashes of a typed `https://`. Rule: ligatures off in mono contexts; now in the shared stylesheet.
- **2026-10-18, Zero To BNS and BNS Lookup, the price shown was the seller's.** A listing read as 103,921 STX when the buyer pays 106,000. Rule: the price the reader pays comes first.
- **2026-09-07 to 2026-10-18, footer cross-promotion removed.** sBTC Deposit Recovery, Zero To BNS and BNS Lookup each removed "Check out other sidekicks" and assert its absence. Rule: a single-purpose tool's footer carries its repo link only.
- **2026-10-02, zero_to homepage, uneven tile grid.** A nowrap line inside `1fr` columns stretched its column; `repeat(3, minmax(0, 1fr))` fixed it. A hash anchor scrolled before the texts above it loaded. Reference notes in components.md.
- **2026-10-19, Zero to Claiming, hidden fee step.** v34 hides the Fees step for a non-admin instead of showing it struck through; shape-disabled steps stay visible as not applicable. Candidate: role-impossible steps are hidden.
- **Fonts.** The system originally used Matter and Matter Mono (commercial, Displaay Type Foundry, licensed to Stacks Labs); they were removed so deployments outside that licence are safe. Instrument Sans and JetBrains Mono are SIL OFL, Open Sauce Sans is free. Rule: do not reintroduce a commercial face without confirming the licence covers the host.
