# Security audit of contract A.0 (signer-manager-stx-payout), 2026-09-24

Scope: `contracts/signer-manager-stx-payout.clar` at the 2026-09-24 revision before this audit (structure hash `ecdd801e95d9863412d7f845b8d0b26b46363532066a96ff9d115ef56da2b4d3`), read in full together with the mock callees, the deployed `dlmm-core-v-1-1` source and the Max 500 parent. Framework: the aibtc `clarity-audit` and `clarity-check` skills (categories logic, security, design, gas, standards; severities critical, high, medium, low), the aibtc `clarity-patterns` notes on `tx-sender` versus `contract-caller` and `as-contract`, and the stxer guidance on post-conditions and fork simulation. Method: one independent adversarial pass by a reviewer who had not seen the contract, then verification of every finding against the source and the simnet suites. Static analysis plus simnet; no mainnet fork run yet (see `simulations/`).

Result: 0 critical, 0 high, 3 medium, 7 low, 3 informational. Both reserve invariants (sBTC balance covers `earned-fees + withdrawal-liability + total-unclaimed-rewards + credited-refunds + total-pending-payouts + pending-conversion-sats`; STX balance covers `ustx-liability`) hold on every traced path, including private-function error paths inside `settle-many` and `payout-many`, both legs of `crystallize`, `abandon-epoch`, fill-or-fail `convert`, and `payout-core` after its reorder. The medium findings are liveness and trust-surface, not extraction.

## Checked and found correct (summary of the reviewer's notes)

Reserve accounting on every write path; deficit booking equals the shortfall and is repaid by the next pull before crediting; `book-stx-settlement`, both `crystallize` legs and `convert` are reserve-neutral; payouts decrement exactly what they transfer after the transfer; batch entries never `try!` and every write follows the external call; `open-epoch` is always `convert-epoch` or `convert-epoch + 1`; a closed epoch always has `sats-total > 0`; the DLMM quote is line for line the core's arithmetic and calls the core's own `get-bin-price`; the Velar quote uses the same `find-dx` with the pool's argument order and passes the exact quote as `amt-out-desired`; fallback runs only when nothing moved; a partial DLMM fill is caught as a public error; all admin and converter gates present; `update-admin` self-removal guard intact; route principals are constants; third-party `payout` cannot redirect funds; every `as-contract?` allowance equals what the callee pulls; no `block-height`; error codes u1001 to u1031 unique; no reentrancy surface.

## stxer guidance applied

- Operator transactions that call `convert`, `abandon-epoch`, `payout-many` and the Jing functions move assets from the contract principal, not from the operator. In wallets, use contract-principal post-conditions (the contract sends at most the tranche in sBTC; for STX payouts the contract sends at most the summed `get-stx-claimable`), or the fork simulation's `Deny` mode to see which assets move before broadcasting.
- Run `simulations/fork-stxer.mjs` (guards, then `--lifecycle`) against the exact deploy file before mainnet; it is the only step that executes the real DLMM trait-argument path and the real pox-5 per-staker read count.
